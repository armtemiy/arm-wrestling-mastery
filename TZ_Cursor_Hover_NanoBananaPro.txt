CUSTOM CURSOR HOVER STATE - TECHNICAL BRIEF

PROJECT CONTEXT
Project name: Armtemiy Armwrestling Training
Website style: The Underground Forge - dark, aggressive, powerful, brutalist
Target audience: Athletes, strength-focused people
Primary color: Crimson Red hsl(5 85% 60%) - hex #E63946
Secondary color: Orange-Red hsl(15 90% 50%) - hex #FF4500
Background: Warm Black hsl(15 8% 6%)

TASK DESCRIPTION
Create a custom mouse cursor for hover state (when hovering over buttons and links).
The cursor should represent armwrestling grip, struggle, connection.

CONCEPT: HAND GRIP HOOK
A minimalist outline of two hands in armwrestling hook grip position.
Think of it as a stylized logo or hieroglyph - instantly recognizable as a grip.

VISUAL REQUIREMENTS
Size: 48x48 pixels (export at 192x192 for retina displays)
Format: PNG with transparent background
Style: Minimalist silhouette, contour lines only
Hot spot position: Center of the grip (24, 24)

DESIGN ELEMENTS
Element 1: Left arm/forearm outline - curved into a hook shape
Element 2: Right arm/forearm outline - mirror image, meeting in center
Element 3: The connection point where hands lock together (hook grip mechanism)
Element 4 (optional): Slight tilt or dynamic angle to show tension

Line thickness: 2-3 pixels
Line ends: Sharp or slightly rounded
Color: Pure #E63946 (Crimson Red) or gradient to #FF4500 for second arm

STYLE GUIDELINES
This is NOT a realistic drawing. No fingers, muscles, or detailed anatomy.
Only the essential outline - like a sports logo or icon.
The shape should read instantly as "two hands gripping" even at small size.
Think of it as a geometric symbol, not an illustration.

MOOD REFERENCES
Think about: minimalist sports logos, olympic pictograms, emoji-style simplicity, tribal symbols
The design should be: abstract, powerful, instantly readable, iconic

WHAT TO AVOID
Avoid: realistic muscles or fingers, complex curves, too many details, cartoonish style
The grip must be recognizable from silhouette alone
Do not make it look like a handshake - this is a competitive grip, not cooperation

COLOR SPECIFICATION
Primary arm: #E63946 (hsl(5 85% 60%))
Secondary arm (optional): #FF4500 (hsl(15 90% 50%))
Gradient or solid color - both acceptable
Glow (optional): hsl(5 85% 60% 0.3) with 30% opacity

DELIVERABLE
One PNG file: cursor-hover.png
Size: 192x192px minimum (for 2x retina scaling)
Transparent background

ADDITIONAL NOTES
The cursor should feel powerful - like two forces locked in struggle.
It represents competition, strength, and the armwrestling sport.
Keep it abstract and geometric - think logo, not illustration.
The design language is Industrial Brutalist - dark red, aggressive, minimalist.
Less detail is better - if it reads clearly at 48x48 pixels, you succeeded.

VISUAL REFERENCE FOR SHAPE
Imagine the letter "X" but the top parts are curved like hooks
Or imagine two "C" shapes facing each other and interlocking
The hands should meet in the center - that's the focal point
